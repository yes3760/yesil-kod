SOLUTION

You cannot optimise what you do not measure. Yeşil Kod works in four steps.

1

Find

Walks the syntax tree and flags thirty energy-wasting patterns, line by line.

2

Fix

Rewrites six of them itself, touching only the changed lines.

3

Verify

Runs both versions and rejects the fix unless the outputs are identical.

4

Measure

Reads CPU energy counters and converts the gain to carbon.

METHOD

We measure the gain without overstating it.

Carbon accounting

SCI = (E × I + M) / R
  • Emeasured CPU energy (powermetrics on Mac, RAPL on Linux)
  • ITürkiye grid, 341 gCO₂e/kWh
  • Membodied hardware emissions (next phase)
  • Rper request

Honest limits

  • Only CPU energy is measured; memory, network and cooling are excluded. The gain shown is a lower bound.
  • Every result is the mean of 5 repeats; caches are cleared before every request.
  • In estimated mode, ratios are more reliable than absolute values.
