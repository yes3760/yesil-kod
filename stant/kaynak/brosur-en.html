WHY IT MATTERS

Code has an invisible carbon cost. Yeşil Kod makes it visible.

Data-centre electricity use is expected to grow from 415 TWh in 2024 to ~945 TWh in 2030 (IEA). Part of that energy feeds waste nobody notices: loops that repeat the same work thousands of times, needless database queries, results computed over and over.

1

Find

Walks the syntax tree and flags six energy-wasting patterns, line by line.

2

Fix

Rewrites four of them itself: batched query, set lookup, loop hoisting, caching.

3

Verify

Runs both versions and rejects the fix unless the outputs are identical.

4

Measure

Reads CPU energy counters and converts the gain to carbon on the Türkiye grid.

Carbon accounting

SCI = (E × I + M) / R
  • E measured CPU energy
  • I Türkiye grid, 341 gCO₂e/kWh
  • M embodied emissions (next phase)
  • R per request

Honest limits

  • Only CPU energy is measured; memory, network and cooling are excluded. The gain is a lower bound.
  • Caches are cleared before every request.
  • The engine currently supports Python.
